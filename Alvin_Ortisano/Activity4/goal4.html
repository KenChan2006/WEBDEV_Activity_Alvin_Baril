My Top 3 Movies of All Time

Harry Potter and the Sorcerer’s Stone

This is the movie that started it all. I love how magical and heartwarming it feels,
especially when Harry discovers the wizarding
world and starts his journey at Hogwarts. The friendship, adventure, and magic make this
movie really special and nostalgic for me.

Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban

This is one of my favorites because it feels darker,
deeper, and more mysterious. I love the story of Sirius Black,
the Time-Turner, and how Harry learns more about his past.
The movie has such a beautiful atmosphere,
and it really shows how the characters are growing up.

Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows: Part 2

The perfect ending to the whole Harry Potter journey.
It’s emotional, intense, and unforgettable.
I love the final battle, the sacrifices,
and seeing everything come together after all the adventures
from the previous movies. It’s definitely a movie that makes me feel nostalgic every time I watch it.

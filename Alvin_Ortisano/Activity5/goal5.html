Alvin's Recipe for the Best Spaghetti

Ingredients

For the spaghetti:

  • 400 g spaghetti noodles
  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • Water for boiling

For the sauce:

  • 500 g ground beef
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can tomato sauce
  • 1 cup tomato ketchup
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 cup sliced hotdogs
  • ½ cup grated cheese

Instructions

  1. Boil water in a large pot and add salt.
  2. Add the spaghetti noodles and cook until tender. Drain and set aside.
  3. Heat the cooking oil in a large pan and sauté the onion and garlic.
  4. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
  5. Add the sliced hotdogs and cook for a few minutes.
  6. Pour in the tomato sauce and ketchup, then add the sugar, salt, and black pepper.
  7. Simmer the sauce for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  8. Add some grated cheese and mix until the sauce becomes rich and flavorful.
  9. Place the cooked spaghetti on a plate and pour the sauce on top.
  10. Sprinkle more cheese over the spaghetti and serve hot.
